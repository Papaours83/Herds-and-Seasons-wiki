## Cooking

In a furnace, a smoker or on a campfire. The quality of raw meat is kept in all three.

| Raw | Cooked |
|---|---|
| Raw Venison | Cooked Venison |
| Raw Boar Meat | Cooked Boar Meat |
| Raw Duck | Roast Duck |
| Raw Goose | Roast Goose |

## No recipe

Fertilized eggs are laid by female birds. DNA samples are taken with the syringe. Goat milk comes from a female dairy goat, using a glass bottle.
